# Code of Conduct

FractawOS is an open-source technical community.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, discuss technical disagreements constructively, and avoid harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, or deliberate disruption.

Technical criticism is welcome.

Personal hostility is not.

Disagreements should focus on code, architecture, evidence, requirements, and project goals.

Maintainers may moderate or remove content that significantly disrupts productive collaboration.

Serious or repeated violations may result in restricted participation in project spaces.
